Privacy policy

Ma mémorisation
Français · العربية · Support

In short

Without an account, your progress and your notes stay on your phone. The app only sends anonymous statistics (your phone’s country and language), which you can turn off in your Profile.

With an account (optional), we only keep your email address, your progress and your notes, to back them up and get them back on another phone. They are hosted in the European Union, in Sweden.

The app is free. Donations and garden shop scenery go through Apple’s App Store or Google Play: we never see your bank details, your name or your Apple or Google account.

No ads, no selling of data, no tracking of what you do in the app or anywhere else.

Who is responsible for your data

Ma mémorisation is published by Insaf Aberkane, in Belgium, the controller of your data.

The data the app uses

Your progress: your surahs and their folders, the verses memorized, the cycle days, the review dates, the days spent with the Quran, the style of each tree, the scenery bought in the garden shop, the scenery you chose and your settings. Your notes (personal, on verses and on surahs) and the colours you put on verses and words are saved with it.

Without an account, this information is stored only on your phone.

With an account, it is also stored in the cloud, with your email address. Each time you sign in, our host also keeps technical information (such as the IP address and the time of sign-in) to keep the service secure.

The app doesn’t ask for your name, your location, your contacts or your photos.

Why

Your email address is used only to send you the sign-in code and to recognize your account (or, with Sign in with Apple, only to recognize your account).

Your progress is used only to back it up and get it back on your devices.

This data is processed because you chose to create an account to use the cloud backup.

Donations and the garden shop

Donations and garden shop scenery (trees, bushes, houses and sets) are in-app purchases from the App Store (on iPhone and iPad) or Google Play (on Android). Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google, under their own privacy policies (apple.com/legal/privacy and policies.google.com/privacy).

Apple or Google only tells the app what was bought and whether the purchase is valid (the product, the date and a transaction number), so it can thank you and give you your scenery.

Scenery you buy is saved with your progress: on your phone, and also in the cloud if you have an account. The “Restore my purchases” button simply asks Apple or Google for the list of your purchases.

Apple and Google also send us reports without your identity (for example the number of purchases per country), for accounting.

A donation doesn’t unlock anything, and the shop only changes the garden’s scenery: all of the memorization stays complete and free for everyone.

Sign in with Apple

On iPhone and iPad, you can create your account or sign in with “Continue with Apple”, instead of the email code.

Apple then only shares your email address and an identifier specific to Ma mémorisation, to recognize your account. If you choose “Hide My Email”, Apple creates an address that forwards messages to yours: we never see your real address.

The app doesn’t ask Apple for your name, and Apple sees neither your progress nor your notes. Sign-in is handled by Apple under its own privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy).

The newsletter

If you subscribe to our newsletter (at mamemorisation.beehiiv.com), we keep your email address to send you news about the app. It has no link to the app or to your account.

The newsletter is run by Beehiiv, an American company: your email address may therefore be processed in the United States, under its own privacy policy (beehiiv.com/privacy).

Your address is used because you agreed to receive the newsletter. You can unsubscribe at any time with the link at the bottom of each email: your address is then no longer used.

Anonymous statistics

To know how many people use the app and in which countries, the app sends a small signal: once when it’s first opened, once a day when you open it, and with each donation (with its amount).

This signal only contains the country and language set in your phone’s settings, and whether it’s an iPhone or an Android. It contains no identifier, no email and no progress, and the IP address is not stored with these numbers (our host only keeps it for a few days in its technical logs, for security): there is no way to know who sent it.

These numbers are hosted by Supabase in the European Union (in Sweden), and only the Ma mémorisation team can see them, as totals per country.

You can turn these statistics off at any time in your Profile (“Anonymous statistics”).

Who has access

No one but you can read your data from the app: the database refuses any access to another account’s data.

Two services help us run the account: Supabase, which hosts the database and the accounts in the European Union (in Stockholm, Sweden), and Gmail (Google), which sends the email with your sign-in code: your email address therefore also goes through Google, under its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

Apple or Google handles payments for donations and shop purchases (see “Donations and the garden shop”), and Apple handles Sign in with Apple (see “Sign in with Apple”).

Your data is never sold, rented or shared for advertising.

How long

Your data stays in the cloud as long as your account exists.

When you delete your account, your email address, your progress and your notes are erased from the database right away. Any technical backup copies kept by the host then disappear on their own.

The data stored on your phone stays there until you delete the app.

Your rights

You can view, correct, export or delete your data at any time.

Export: Profile → My data → Backup to a file → Export.

Delete: Profile → Account → Delete my account.

For any other request, you can write to us. If you think your rights are not respected, you can also contact the data protection authority of your country: in Belgium, the Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be); in France, the CNIL (cnil.fr).

Listening to recitations

To let you listen to the Quran, the app downloads the recitations (one file per verse) from everyayah.com, a free recitation website. As with any website, it then sees your phone’s IP address and the files requested (the reciter and the verses). The app sends it nothing else: not your email, not your progress, no identifier.

Downloaded files stay on your phone, so you can listen again without internet. You can delete them at any time in the listening settings.

Links to Instagram and Facebook

The “Follow us” buttons open our Instagram and Facebook pages outside the app. What you do there then depends on those services’ privacy policies: the app sends them nothing.

Security

Exchanges between the app and the cloud are encrypted. There is no password: you sign in with a temporary code sent by email, or with Apple on iPhone and iPad.

Changes

If the app changes the way it uses your data, this page will be updated and the date below will change.
